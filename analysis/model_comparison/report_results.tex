\subsection{Speaker-level pitch normalization}

We evaluated frozen HuBERT and wav2vec2 classifiers on the same 1,429
RAVDESS recordings and 24 leave-one-speaker-out folds under each audio
condition. For each seed, we pooled the held-out predictions across folds
before computing unweighted average recall (UAR) and weighted average recall
(WAR, equivalent to accuracy). Table~\ref{tab:matched-pitch-results} reports
the mean and sample standard deviation over seeds 42, 123, and 999.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Matched frozen-encoder classification on RAVDESS. Scores are in
  percent. Both normalization conditions use a robust speaker-level Praat
  pitch estimate.}
  \label{tab:matched-pitch-results}
  \begin{tabular}{llrr}
    \hline
    Encoder & Audio condition & UAR & WAR \\
    \hline
    HuBERT & Original & $58.63 \pm 0.79$ & $59.60 \pm 0.51$ \\
           & Full shift & $58.54 \pm 0.62$ & $59.32 \pm 0.39$ \\
           & Capped at $\pm 4$ semitones & $59.06 \pm 0.62$ & $59.79 \pm 0.63$ \\
    wav2vec2 & Original & $43.62 \pm 0.37$ & $44.65 \pm 0.39$ \\
             & Full shift & $44.05 \pm 1.06$ & $44.90 \pm 0.99$ \\
             & Capped at $\pm 4$ semitones & $44.09 \pm 0.38$ & $45.18 \pm 0.32$ \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Capping the shift increased mean UAR by 0.44 percentage points for HuBERT and
0.46 percentage points for wav2vec2 relative to original audio. The paired
speaker-resampling intervals were $[-1.54, 2.28]$ and $[-1.47, 2.55]$
percentage points, respectively. Both include zero. These exploratory
estimates are conditional on the fixed predictions and training seeds and
do not account for model retraining or the overlap among LOSO training sets.
The normalized conditions used an unlabeled batch from each speaker to
estimate that speaker's baseline, including the held-out speaker. The
comparison therefore evaluates batch speaker calibration.

\subsection{Layer-wise HuBERT probing}

On the separate 1,440-recording original-audio experiment, layer 7 had the
highest mean validation UAR (71.34\%). Its pooled held-out UAR was
$68.45 \pm 0.48$\%, and WAR was $69.10 \pm 0.43$\%. Layer 6 had the highest
observed held-out UAR among the 12 probes ($69.38 \pm 0.31$\%), compared
with $59.92 \pm 0.95$\% for the final Transformer layer. Layer 7 is the
validation-selected result. Layer 6's held-out maximum describes the
observed layer profile. The probe experiment used the full original-audio
cohort, while Table~\ref{tab:matched-pitch-results} used the matched subset.
